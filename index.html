HELP CENTER

Need help creating your resume?

How do I change my information?

Open the Resume Builder and edit the fields. Your resume updates automatically.

How do I change template?

Select any template from the Design page or use the template dropdown inside the builder.

How do I add my photo?

Choose a profile image from your computer. The image will immediately appear on your resume.

How do I download?

Click Download / Print and select Save as PDF in the browser print window.

Can I change colors?

Yes. Select one of the theme colors in the Resume Builder.

Can I edit everything?

Yes. Name, job title, contact information, profile, experience, education and skills can be changed.
